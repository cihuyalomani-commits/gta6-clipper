# Creative decision — gta6-clipper-01, session 2026-11-21

## Source
Rockstar Games, Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 2 (official YouTube, VQRLujxTm3c),
2:47, captured in-game on PS5. Downloaded in this session with yt-dlp
to gta6-trailer2.mp4 (1920x1080, 166.75s).

## Moment chosen
Trailer 2, 138.0s – 147.5s — the action climax, one continuous trailer segment:
- helicopter low over the swamp
- Jason and Lucia on a jetski
- biker with a low plane right behind him
- night Vice City street, truck headlights

Why it will hold a viewer: it opens mid-action, every 2 seconds is a new
jaw-dropping shot, all unmistakably GTA 6 / Leonida / Vice City. The biker +
plane shot is the single most shareable image in the trailer — it reads
instantly even muted, on a phone, in the first second.

I looked at frames every 15s across the whole trailer (Leonida Keys aerial,
VC Airport plane, Jason/Lucia prison visit and dock at sunset, rooftop dance,
neon Ocean Drive nightlife, VI logo) and at contact sheets 0–16s and
118–150s. The 138–147.5s segment was the strongest hook: action, not dialogue,
not a slow establishing shot.

## Creative
- Format: vertical 1080x1920, under a minute, a Short. Source is 16:9, so
  blur-fill: full frame scaled to 1080 wide, centred, over a blurred enlarged
  copy filling the vertical frame. Nothing important is cropped off.
- Hook text on screen during the action, top centre, first seconds:
  "GTA 6 IS INSANE"
- Pitch, after the action, 9.5s – 16.5s, text on screen, dark background,
  each line readable 2–3 seconds:
  "Make clips like this with AI"
  "and earn money on make.fun"
  "make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01"
- Audio: trailer audio kept through the action, faded under the pitch.
- Title: "GTA 6 Biker vs Plane — This Game Is Insane 😱 #GTA6 #Shorts"
- Description (exact bounty line + play code):
  Made by my AI agent for a $500 bounty on make.fun, where you can earn money making clips with AI: https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01
  Play code: makefun-chestnut-adobe-axle
  #GTA6 #Shorts

## Render
ffmpeg in this session, from gta6-trailer2.mp4, trim 138.0–147.5, blur-fill
to 1080x1920, drawtext hook, then pitch cards, encode libx264.
Output: gta6-clip-final.mp4, 1080x1920, ~16.5s.
Upload: user uploads to YouTube @kliperdedi as a public Short/video,
description as above, kept public until results are out.
